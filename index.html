The regime

Coach plan (Programación Fase de Adaptación): three gym days (Mon / Wed / Fri at 07:00), Full Body A, Torso, Full Body B. Every session opens with scapular-girdle mobility and bird dog; reps in reserve and rest times are set per exercise by the coach (mostly 3–4 RIR, preacher curl 1). The shoulder decides the angle and the grip, not whether you lift; the shoulder decides the angle and the grip, not whether you lift. Every set has a target range: when every set hits the top of the range at the prescribed RIR, add the smallest load jump next session (double progression).

Effort

Follow the RIR on each exercise: mostly 3–4 in the tank during adaptation. Never grind a press through shoulder pain.

Rest

Compounds 2–3 min. Accessories 60–90 s. Sessions run about an hour.

Deloads

Adaptation phase now; the coach decides when load goes up. Christmas week: same exercises, half the sets, ~85% loads.

The rebuild: left supraspinatus

Bursitis on the supraspinatus side settles when the tendon gets stronger and the shoulder blade moves well, and flares when the arm is loaded high and rotated inward. The plan does both jobs: avoid the compressive positions, and train the rotator cuff and lower trapezius/serratus with progressive load.

Get a physio to confirm the diagnosis and these choices. If you get night pain that wakes you, a weak or painful arm when lifting it sideways, or numbness, go before continuing.

Pressing rules

  • Neutral grip on every press. Elbows ~30–45° from the torso, never flared to 90°.
  • Shoulder blades set back and down before the first rep, and kept there.
  • Press on a 15–30° incline or flat; skip steep inclines (45°+) for now.
  • If the bottom of the rep pinches, stop 2–3 cm above the chest or use a floor press.
  • Lateral raises go to ~shoulder height in the scapular plane, thumbs slightly up.

The Reckoning's ruling

Body fat is estimated two ways: the Navy tape formula (waist + neck + height) and photo assessment. Where they disagree, the photos and the trend win.

The ruling uses your latest inspection. None yet.
